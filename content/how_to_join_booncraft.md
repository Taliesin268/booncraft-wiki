## Requirements
- Minecraft **Java Edition** — version **1.21.5 (supports 1.20.7–1.21.7)**
- Internet connection (wired if possible)
- Optional: Discord for community chat — https://discord.gg/B6bSs5fUgD

## Steps
1. Open the Minecraft Launcher and set your installation to **1.21.8 (supports 1.20.7–1.21.8)**.
2. Go to **Multiplayer → Add Server**.
   - **Server Name:** Booncraft
   - **Server Address:** play.boon-craft.com
3. Click **Done**, select **Booncraft**, hit **Join Server**.
4. On first login, read the welcome (`/rules`) and say hi in chat.

## Optional Client Mods
- **Voice Chat:** [Simple Voice Chat](https://modrinth.com/plugin/simple-voice-chat) (install to listen or talk in-game)

**Having trouble?** → See [[faq_and_troubleshooting|FAQ & Troubleshooting]].

## Joining with Bedrock Edition (Geyser Users)

### For Mobile (Pocket Edition)

1. Launch **Minecraft Bedrock Edition** on your device.
2. Click **Play**.
3. Click **Servers**.
4. Click **Add Server**.
5. Enter the following:
   - **Server Name:** BoonCraft
   - **Server Address:** play.boon-craft.com
   - **Port:** 19132
6. Click **Done** and then join the server!

### For Console Editions (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch)

1. Make sure both your mobile device and console are connected to the same internet network.
2. On your phone, install [BedrockTogether](https://bedrocktogether.net/).
3. Open BedrockTogether and enter:
   - **Server IP:** play.boon-craft.com
   - **Port:** 19132
4. Start BedrockTogether, then on your console, check your world/server list.
5. Join the BoonCraft server and enjoy!

> **Note:** Console joining requires a mobile device to act as a proxy using BedrockTogether. This is a common workaround for joining custom servers on consoles.
